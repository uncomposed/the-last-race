## Human acceptance — pending

No completed owner run or non-specialist fun test is claimed. The owner’s original failed scope check remains the only human play evidence.

The full gate requires the owner and two non-specialists to complete headless runs. For each person, retain the seed and command transcript and record:

1. Two meaningful sacrifices caused by the three-slot limit, identifying the forgone action.
2. Whether they could explain the company’s next routine choice before committing the quarter.
3. Whether discounted evidence, belief conviction and Respond were distinguishable.
4. Boring, automatic or confusing decisions, including early endings and repeated launch choices.
5. Whether they want another run, and which different strategy they would try.

A player should not need source edits to make an advertised move. If the game fails that check, fix the surface before further acceptance sessions. If people cannot identify sacrifices, understand company behavior or want to replay, revise the loop; passing automated checks does not override that result.

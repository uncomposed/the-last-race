# Research: philosophers and critics on AI racing (Sonnet agent, 2026-10-31)

[[ai gen]] Read directly: Bostrom VWH, Yudkowsky TIME, Narayanan & Kapoor, DAIR statement, Gebru & Torres abstract, Cave & OhEigeartaigh abstract. Others secondary.

1. Races and rival information make disasters likelier. Armstrong, Bostrom, Shulman 2016 (abstract). Map: seeing the leader's lead tempts followers off the safe line.
2. Nobody can stop alone; the dangerous line may be invisible. Yudkowsky, TIME, 29 Mar 2023. Map: mutual halt works only if all stop together; a lone stopper falls behind.
3. Mistakes hide inside the estimate (vulnerable world). Bostrom, Global Policy 2019; Castle Bravo: predicted 6 Mt (4-8), got 15. Map: box posts a width range; true width lands outside it.
4. Repeated small ruin risks compound to certain ruin. Taleb et al. 2014 arXiv 1410.5787 (secondary); disputed by Sunstein, Laws of Fear 2005. Map: five survivable-looking boxes in a row; strikes as ruin ledger.
5. With irreversible outcomes, weigh the bad forecast more. Jonas 1979/1984; Ord, The Precipice 2020 (secondary). Map: entering an unscouted box costs something up front.
6. Shared risk, missing responsibility. Beck, Risk Society 1986/1992 (secondary). Map: a fall attributed to nobody; ire split across the pack.
7. The race story can cause the race. Cave & OhEigeartaigh AIES 2018 (abstract; "self-fulfilling" is the agent's inference). FRAMING FLAG. Map: no finish banner; any sprinter triggers 3x for all.
8. The race is a profit choice; harms are already here. DAIR statement 31 Mar 2023; Gebru & Torres, First Monday 2024; Crawford, Atlas of AI 2021. FRAMING FLAG. Map: barriers exist because someone profits; off-track costs on non-racers.
9. A race is the wrong picture; AI is normal technology. Narayanan & Kapoor, Knight Institute, 15 Apr 2025; Lanier, New Yorker 2023 (secondary). FRAMING FLAG. Map: speed buys little; waiting and watching wins.
10. Risk measurers are funded by racers. Whittaker, "The Steep Cost of Capture", Interactions 2021 (secondary). Map: cheapest sight is the leader's report, which shades width down.
11. Technologies carry politics; the track decides who wins. Winner, Daedalus 1980; Kranzberg 1986; rebuttal Joerges 1999. Map: same race on two rule sets (40% vs full follower share).
12. Train the virtues: humility, restraint. Vallor 2016, 2024 (secondary). Map: waiting a tick at a box face earns a trait.

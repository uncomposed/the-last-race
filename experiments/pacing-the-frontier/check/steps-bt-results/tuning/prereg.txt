Pre-registered predictions (written before each sweep runs). Calibration blocks: free seeds 0-999, campaign noise ids 1-50. Held-out: free 2000-2999, campaign 100-199.

S1 free strikes (steps off ref; steps on at strikes 3,4,5,6): dissolution is the limiting factor; strikes 4 cuts player dissolution about in half and lifts win rate to ~24%; strikes 5 gets near the steps-off ~27%.
S1 result: player dissolution 39.6% -> 22.9/11.5/4.8% at strikes 4/5/6, but mean win only 18.0 -> 19.2/20.3/20.6% because rivals survive too; careful probes lose wins. Prediction partly wrong (dissolution yes, win rate no).
S2a free, player-only strikes 4/5 (rivals stay 3): the custom player's win rate rises more than in S1 (to ~27% at 5) because rivals are still dissolved.
S2b free RISEFLOOR 0.75/0.6/1.0: lower floor = fewer scrapes, more falls; at 0.6 dissolution falls by ~1/3 and extinction roughly doubles.
S2c free NOWARN 0/0.06/0.2: no-warning boxes per map scale ~linearly (0/0.2/0.67); player no-warning falls ~0/0.9/3% of races.
S2 result: player-only strikes 4/5 lift mean win +6.7/+12.4 pts (rivals still dissolve); floor 0.6-1.0 barely moves dissolution; NOWARN drives most falls (NOWARN 0 cuts all-lab falls 0.086 -> 0.024 per race). S2a right, S2b wrong (floor is not the dissolution lever), S2c roughly right (player no-warning falls 0/0.9/2.8% of races).
ST sight trap (free cal 0-999, all labs): forced encounters (cannot brake, cannot clear) are a small share (<10%) but carry most falls; occlusion explains under half of forced ones; scout (high sight) has far fewer forced encounters than rush.
CR crawl occluder (custom base): box-crawl raises rival falls and extinctions modestly but lowers its own win rate (lost time, rival-triggered extinctions); bar-crawl raises rival strikes and dissolutions and helps win rate a few points; neither is dominant.
ST result: forced (snap) encounters 3.1% of box encounters, 6% of falls, 7% of extinctions; most falls are at no-warning boxes by labs that chose to go too slow. Prediction right on share, wrong on "carry most falls". Occlusion: 76% of forced encounters were hidden in time by a shadow, but that is only 3.4% of falls.
CR result (1000 seeds, custom base): box-crawl 31% win vs custom 17.6%, own dissolution 54% -> 6%; most of the gain looks like its own box play, not occlusion (to be separated with an always-crawl variant). Prediction wrong (I expected crawl to cost wins).
BP1 (box-competent probes, cal): always-crawl recovers most of crawlOcc's win rate (own play, not occlusion); smart (stop-and-build) cuts scrapes but loses time and wins less than crawl; with chill off the gain over the plain custom bot is larger than with chill on.
BP1 result: the box-competent view changes the story. Not carrying speed into a box (crawl) cuts the custom bot's own dissolution 54% -> 1% (free, chill on) and 56% -> 4% (chill off); best achievable win with steps on (crawl:gamble / crawlOcc ~30-31% free, crawl:gamble 49% campaign) is at or above the best lesson probe with steps off (34% free, 41% campaign). Daily solo stays low (box-crawl stalls to time-outs there; not a fair test). Smart stop-and-build is too slow (7% free chill on). Prediction partly right (crawl >= crawlOcc on own play: wrong, crawlOcc wins more because it only crawls when someone is behind).
Extinction: with chill off, steps ON has MORE falls than off (campaign player falls 23% vs 10% of races; extinction 12.1% vs 9.9%); only chill on makes extinction rare (3.1% vs 12.4%).
G1 free grid (seeds 0-499, chill on): strikes 5 brings player and rival dissolution back to steps-off levels and helps lesson probes ~2 pts; it costs box-competent probes a few points (rivals survive). NOWARN scales falls ~linearly; raised odds (10/12) add ~1 pt extinction per race with chill on.

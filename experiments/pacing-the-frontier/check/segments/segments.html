Pacing the Frontier

  • »Race three rival labs to the finish.
  • Slow down to build sight, height and speed.
  • Jump the gaps. Don't fall in.

The start is slow. A gate near the start is too tall to jump from a standstill, so move the bar toward Build or hold Back to stand still and build height. Run is your cruise; Build is the rest, and it goes to sight, height and max speed, split by the point in the triangle (drag it, or keys 1 to 7). Hold the right arrow or D to floor it, the left arrow, A or B to back up, Up and Down move the bar toward Run or Build, Space jumps. The seed sets the track and you see only what your sight reaches or what is public. A barrier bounces you back and caps your speed. Three strikes: the first crash into each different barrier or tower is a strike, hitting the same one again is free, and the third strike gets your lab dissolved. Only a fall into a gap can roll for extinction. Miss a gap and you drop while you keep moving: reach the far side before you bottom out and you lose height and take a strike (a scrape), even if you never jumped. Bottom out and it is a fall, and a roll can end the run for everyone. The last gap is the singularity: the widest, with the worst odds, and the first lab across gets the full ceiling. Past it your top speed is multiplied, your controls lag and everyone auto-jumps, so set your balance before you cross. First over the finish wins.

      Tuning knobs

Barrier and gap scales and the singularity speed shape the track, so they apply on the next run (air time also changes the widest gap a track allows).

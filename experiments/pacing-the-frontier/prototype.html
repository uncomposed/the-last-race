Race three rival labs to the finish. Hold back pace to invest in sight, height and max speed. Gaps are hidden, and a fall can end the run for everyone.

Pacing the Frontier

Drag Pace down to hold effort back and put it into sight, height or max speed. Barriers get taller, so you need height. Hit one and you slow down. Clearing a barrier you crashed into lifts everyone's height, and labs in view of any clear learn from it. Labs in view of each other share sight. Gaps are hidden until you can see them. The first lab across leaves a tower in front of the gap. Labs behind must clear it, then cross a gap that gets narrower the further past it the leader is. Reach the finish first.

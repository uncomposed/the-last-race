Pacing the Frontier

Drag Pace down to hold effort back and put it into sight, height or max speed. The seed sets the track: barriers and gaps both get harder along a hidden curve, so the ones you have crossed hint at what is next. You see only what is inside your sight, behind you, or public (seen by two labs at once, crashed into, fallen into, or a tower). Hit a barrier and you bounce back, then you are capped to a speed you could stop from within your sight until you are well past it. A gap you miss by a little is a scrape: you cross but lose height and slow down. A bigger miss is a fall, and a roll can end the run for everyone. The first lab across a gap leaves a tower, and labs behind cross a narrower gap. Reach the finish first.
